# Brazil — LGPD

## 1. Regulation Identity

- **Regulation:** Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados Pessoais (LGPD)
- **Law:** Lei nº 13.709, de 14 August 2018
- **Jurisdiction:** Brazil
- **Regulator:** Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD)
- **Regulation type:** Federal privacy/data protection law
- **Primary law status:** In force, subject to provision-specific amendments and ANPD regulations

---

## 2. Primary Sources

- Lei nº 13.709/2018 (LGPD)
- ANPD regulations and resolutions
- ANPD Regulation on International Transfer of Personal Data — Resolution CD/ANPD nº 19/2024
- ANPD Regulation on Administrative Sanctions — Resolution CD/ANPD nº 4/2023

---

## 3. Territorial / Applicability Scope

LGPD applies to processing of personal data when:

1. The processing operation is carried out in Brazil;
2. The processing activity is intended to offer goods or services, or process data of individuals located in Brazil; or
3. The personal data was collected in Brazil.

Certain statutory exceptions apply, including specified processing for journalism, artistic, academic, public security, national defense, state security, and criminal-investigation purposes.

---

## 4. Covered Actors

- **Data Subject (Titular):** natural person to whom personal data relates.
- **Controller (Controlador):** person or entity responsible for decisions concerning processing.
- **Processor (Operador):** processes personal data on behalf of the controller.
- **Data Protection Officer / DPO (Encarregado):** designated privacy contact required subject to LGPD/ANPD rules.
- **ANPD:** Brazilian data protection authority.

---

## 5. Covered Data

- Personal data
- Sensitive personal data
- Anonymized data, generally outside LGPD when anonymization cannot reasonably be reversed
- Data concerning children and adolescents receives special protection

Personal data includes information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

---

## 6. Lawful Processing / Legal Bases

LGPD does **not** use a single "consent required" model.

Processing of ordinary personal data may rely on statutory legal bases including:

- Consent
- Compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation
- Processing by public administration for public policies
- Research by research bodies, subject to statutory safeguards
- Exercise of rights in judicial, administrative, or arbitration proceedings
- Protection of life or physical safety
- Healthcare-related protection of health
- Legitimate interests
- Credit protection
- Other statutory bases provided by LGPD

Sensitive personal data has a separate and more restrictive set of legal bases.

---

## 7. Consent Requirements

Where consent is used:

- Consent must be free, informed, and unequivocal.
- It must relate to a specific purpose.
- The controller bears responsibility for demonstrating that consent was obtained lawfully.
- Consent cannot be obtained through misleading or abusive mechanisms.
- Authorizations for processing should be distinguishable where required by law.
- Changes to processing purposes may require renewed consent where the original consent no longer covers the new purpose.

---

## 8. When Consent Is Not Required

Consent is not universally required.

LGPD permits processing under other statutory legal bases, including:

- Legal/regulatory obligations
- Public policy/public administration
- Exercise of legal rights
- Protection of life or physical safety
- Health protection
- Legitimate interests
- Credit protection
- Other expressly permitted statutory circumstances

The applicable legal basis depends on the purpose, data category, actor, and context.

---

## 9. Sensitive / Special Data

Sensitive personal data includes data concerning:

- Racial or ethnic origin
- Religious belief
- Political opinion
- Membership in a union or religious, philosophical, or political organization
- Health or sex life
- Genetic or biometric data when linked to a natural person

Sensitive-data processing has a distinct legal-basis framework and additional restrictions.

---

## 10. Children and Adolescents

- Processing of children's and adolescents' personal data must prioritize their best interests.
- Processing children's data generally requires specific and prominent consent from at least one parent or legal guardian.
- Limited statutory exceptions permit collection without that consent, including certain circumstances necessary to contact/protect the child.
- Controllers must provide public information concerning collected data, its use, and rights procedures.

---

## 11. Transparency / Notice

Controllers must provide information concerning processing in a clear and accessible manner.

Transparency should cover relevant matters such as:

- Purpose of processing
- Nature and scope of processing
- Applicable legal basis
- Identity/contact information of responsible parties where required
- Data-subject rights
- Information required by specific LGPD provisions

The transparency requirement is context-dependent and should not be reduced to a single notice template.

---

## 12. Data Subject Rights

Data subjects have rights including:

- Confirmation of whether personal data is being processed
- Access to personal data
- Correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data
- Anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data or data processed unlawfully
- Data portability, subject to applicable regulation and commercial/industrial secrets
- Deletion of personal data processed on the basis of consent, subject to legal exceptions
- Information about public/private entities with which data has been shared
- Information about the possibility of refusing consent and consequences
- Revocation of consent
- Review of certain automated decisions
- Other rights established by LGPD

---

## 13. Withdrawal / Objection / Opt-Out

- Consent may be revoked at any time through a facilitated procedure.
- Revocation does not necessarily invalidate processing already performed lawfully before withdrawal.
- Data subjects may exercise statutory rights concerning processing based on legitimate interests and other legal bases where applicable.
- LGPD does not map directly to the CCPA/CPRA concept of a universal "sale/sharing opt-out".

---

## 14. Retention / Erasure

Personal data should not be retained indefinitely without a lawful purpose.

Processing should end when applicable statutory conditions are met, including:

- Purpose has been achieved;
- Data is no longer necessary or relevant;
- Statutory retention period expires;
- Data subject exercises a deletion right where applicable;
- ANPD determines termination in an applicable circumstance.

LGPD also provides circumstances in which data must be retained despite a deletion request, such as legal or regulatory obligations.

---

## 15. Security / Breach

Controllers and processors must adopt technical and administrative security measures appropriate to protect personal data.

Security measures should address:

- Unauthorized access
- Accidental or unlawful destruction
- Loss
- Alteration
- Communication
- Dissemination

Security obligations apply throughout processing and should consider the nature of the data and processing risks.

Personal-data security incidents may trigger notification obligations to the ANPD and affected data subjects under applicable LGPD/ANPD requirements.

---

## 16. Vendors / Data Processors

- Controllers and processors have distinct roles.
- A processor acts on behalf of the controller.
- Controllers should establish appropriate controls over processing performed by processors.
- Contracts and operational controls should define processing responsibilities, security requirements, confidentiality, and assistance obligations as appropriate.
- Responsibility may depend on the actor's role and the circumstances of the violation.

---

## 17. International Transfers

International transfers are permitted only through mechanisms established by LGPD.

Mechanisms include, among others:

- Transfer to countries/organizations recognized as providing an adequate level of protection
- Appropriate contractual safeguards
- Standard contractual clauses
- Specific contractual clauses
- Global corporate rules
- Other statutory mechanisms, including certain consent and public-interest circumstances

ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD nº 19/2024 regulates international-transfer mechanisms and standard contractual clauses.

ANPD may issue adequacy decisions. The EU was recognized by ANPD as an adequate destination in Resolution CD/ANPD nº 32/2026.

---

## 18. Governance / Accountability

Controllers may have governance obligations including:

- Appointment of an Encarregado/DPO subject to applicable requirements
- Privacy and data-protection governance
- Security measures
- Records/documentation appropriate to processing
- Impact assessments where applicable
- Demonstrable compliance and accountability

ANPD may issue complementary rules and sector-specific guidance.

---

## 19. Enforcement / Penalties

- Enforcement authority: ANPD
- Administrative sanctions may include warnings, fines, publicization of the infraction, blocking or deletion of personal data, suspension of processing, and other statutory measures.
- Fines may reach **2% of a company's revenue in Brazil**, limited to **R$50 million per infraction**, subject to LGPD statutory rules.
- ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD nº 4/2023 establishes the administrative sanctioning and dosimetry framework.

---

## 20. Exemptions / Exceptions

LGPD contains exclusions and special rules for processing including:

- Exclusively journalistic purposes
- Artistic purposes
- Academic purposes, subject to statutory conditions
- Public security
- National defense
- State security
- Activities of investigation and prosecution of criminal offenses

Additional statutory exceptions and sector-specific rules must be evaluated according to context.

---

## 21. Effective Dates / Versioning

LGPD commencement was phased: ANPD's official FAQ records the institutional provisions on 2018-12-28, the remaining provisions (excluding administrative sanctions) on 2020-09-18, and Articles 52–54 on 2021-08-01.

- LGPD enacted: **14 August 2018**
- LGPD entered into force in stages; the main data-processing provisions became applicable in 2020.
- Administrative sanctions under LGPD became applicable from **1 August 2021**.
- ANPD regulations may introduce additional effective dates and transition periods.
- Regulation records must therefore be versioned by:
  - Law/regulation identifier
  - Publication date
  - Effective date
  - Amendment date
  - Applicable ANPD resolutions

---

## 22. Requirement Summary / Ambiguities

### Product-relevant requirements

- Support multiple LGPD legal bases.
- Do not model LGPD as `consent_required = true/false`.
- Distinguish ordinary and sensitive personal data.
- Distinguish controller and processor roles.
- Model children/adolescent processing separately.
- Model statutory rights individually.
- Model international-transfer mechanisms separately from the underlying processing legal basis.
- Track ANPD regulations independently from the LGPD itself.
- Support purpose-, data-category-, actor-, and context-specific requirements.
- Support effective dates and regulatory versions.

### Important interpretation boundaries

- LGPD legitimate interest is not equivalent to GDPR legitimate interest in every implementation detail.
- LGPD sensitive-data legal bases must not be treated as identical to ordinary-data legal bases.
- International-transfer authorization and the underlying lawful basis for processing are separate concepts.
- ANPD regulations can add operational requirements without changing the underlying statutory framework.
- Do not automatically translate LGPD into CCPA/CPRA concepts such as "sale" or "sharing".
