# Regulatory Glossary

This glossary defines the normalized terminology used in the regulatory research.

> **Important:** Similar terms across jurisdictions are not assumed to have the same legal meaning. Jurisdiction-specific legal definitions always take precedence.

## Core Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Personal Data | Information regulated as relating to an identified or identifiable individual under the applicable regime. |
| Personal Information | Jurisdiction-specific term used by laws such as California's privacy framework. |
| Data Subject | GDPR term for the individual to whom personal data relates. |
| Consumer | Individual covered by a consumer privacy law, subject to that law's definition. |
| Data Principal | Individual to whom personal data relates under India's DPDP framework. |
| Controller | Entity determining the purposes and means of processing under GDPR. |
| Processor | Entity processing data on behalf of another regulated entity. |
| Data Fiduciary | Entity determining the purpose and means of processing under India's DPDP framework. |
| Business | California-specific regulated entity category. |
| Service Provider | California-specific entity processing personal information for a business under defined conditions. |
| Vendor | Internal product term for an external organization handling data; not itself a legal classification. |

## Data Categories

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Data Category | Classification of data used by the regulatory model. |
| Sensitive Data | Data receiving additional protection under a particular regime. |
| Special Category Data | GDPR-specific category of specially protected personal data. |
| Children's Data | Data relating to children/minors subject to special legal requirements. |
| Anonymous Data | Data that is not considered personal data under the applicable legal standard. |
| Pseudonymous Data | Data processed so that identification requires additional information. |

## Processing & Legal Basis

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Processing | Operations performed on personal data, such as collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion. |
| Purpose | Reason for which data is processed. |
| Processing Context | Circumstances in which processing occurs, such as website, advertising, analytics, or vendor processing. |
| Legal Basis | Recognized legal ground for processing under a particular regime. |
| Legal Authorization | Internal umbrella concept for the legal mechanism permitting processing. |
| Consent | Individual's legally recognized agreement to processing or another regulated activity. |
| Withdrawal | Revocation of previously provided consent. |
| Legitimate Interest | GDPR legal basis allowing processing where applicable conditions are satisfied. |
| Contractual Necessity | Processing permitted because it is necessary for a contract, where applicable. |
| Legal Obligation | Processing permitted or required to comply with a legal obligation, where applicable. |

## User Choices & Rights

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Opt-Out | User direction to stop or prevent a specified activity where the law provides an opt-out right. |
| Right to Object | Legal right to object to specified processing under applicable conditions. |
| Access | Right to obtain information about personal data or its processing. |
| Correction | Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data. |
| Deletion | Right concerning removal of personal data. |
| Erasure | GDPR term associated with deletion of personal data. |
| Portability | Right to receive or transfer personal data in a legally specified format, where applicable. |
| Restriction | GDPR right concerning limitation of processing in specified circumstances. |
| Appeal | Right or mechanism to challenge a privacy-rights response where applicable. |
| Non-Discrimination | Protection against certain adverse treatment for exercising privacy rights. |

## California Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Sale | California-specific statutory concept with a defined legal meaning. |
| Sharing | California-specific concept concerning certain disclosures for cross-context behavioral advertising. |
| Targeted Advertising | Internal term for advertising based on user activity, interests, or similar information. |
| Preference Signal | Technical signal communicating a user's privacy preference where legally recognized. |

## Transparency & Tracking

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Notice | Information provided about processing, privacy practices, rights, or required disclosures. |
| Privacy Notice | Document or interface describing an organization's privacy practices. |
| Cookie | Technology used to store or access information on a user's device. |
| Similar Technology | Technologies performing functions similar to cookies or device storage/access. |
| Tracking | Monitoring or analyzing user activity through cookies, identifiers, pixels, SDKs, or similar technologies. |
| Essential Technology | Technology necessary for a particular functionality; legal exemption must be determined separately. |

## Retention & Transfers

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Retention | Continued storage or maintenance of personal data. |
| Retention Period | Period during which data may or must be retained. |
| Storage Limitation | Principle limiting retention of personal data. |
| International Transfer | Transfer or access of regulated data across jurisdictions subject to transfer requirements. |
| Adequacy | Recognition that a destination provides an adequate level of protection under an applicable framework. |
| Transfer Safeguard | Legal, contractual, organizational, or technical mechanism supporting an international transfer. |
| Derogation | Specific legal exception permitting a transfer under defined conditions. |

## Applicability & Enforcement

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Applicability Trigger | Condition causing a regulation or requirement to apply. |
| Threshold | Quantitative or qualitative condition affecting applicability. |
| Exemption | Condition under which a requirement does not apply. |
| Exception | Condition modifying or limiting an otherwise applicable requirement. |
| Enforcement Authority | Authority responsible for supervising or enforcing a privacy regime. |
| Administrative Penalty | Administrative monetary or other regulatory sanction. |
| Private Right of Action | Ability of a private party to bring a legal claim where provided by law. |
| Cure Period | Period allowing correction of an alleged violation where legally applicable. |

## Research & Versioning

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Primary Source | Official legal, regulatory, or government source establishing or interpreting a requirement. |
| Independent Authoritative Source | Credible independent source used to cross-check a requirement. |
| Requirement | Normalized representation of a legal obligation, permission, prohibition, right, condition, or exception. |
| Rule | Machine-oriented representation of a requirement with scope, conditions, and exceptions. |
| Conflict | Material disagreement between relevant authoritative sources. |
| Ambiguity | Situation where the applicable requirement cannot be determined confidently without further interpretation or context. |
| Version | Specific legally relevant edition or state of a law, regulation, amendment, or guidance document. |
| Effective Date | Date on which a legal provision becomes effective. |
| Effective Period | Period during which a requirement applies. |

## Non-Equivalent Concepts

The following must **not** be automatically treated as equivalent:

```text
consent != opt-out
withdrawal != objection
withdrawal != deletion
controller != business
controller != data fiduciary
processor != service provider
personal data != personal information
sensitive data != special-category data
sale != sharing
notice != consent
legitimate interest != consent
legal basis != user choice
anonymous != pseudonymous
essential != legally exempt
